Development of a scalable and interoperable Cellular Vehicle-to-Everything (C-V2X) framework designed for real-time intelligent decision support in autonomous mobility systems.

This project focuses on enabling seamless communication between connected and autonomous vehicles, infrastructure, and cloud-based services to support safe and efficient transportation.
